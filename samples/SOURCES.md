# Sample sources and licences

Every sample here is a transcription made for this project of music that is in
the public domain (composed and, where there are words, written well over a
century ago, with authors who died before 1956). The transcriptions, the
engravings in the gallery, and the simple piano left hand in `ode-to-joy.abc`
are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No third-party transcription or edition was copied.
